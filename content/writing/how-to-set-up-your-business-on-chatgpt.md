Someone is already asking ChatGPT which company to hire, whether you ship to their city, and what happens if they want to return something. If you have not claimed the listing, the answer comes from whatever ChatGPT can piece together. [Business Hub](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) is where an owner takes responsibility for that information.

You do not need a ChatGPT Enterprise plan, and you do not need to buy ads. You do need to be the owner, or someone the owner has authorized.

## Claim the listing before you change anything

Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub from the sidebar. Search for the business by name and choose the listing that is actually yours. ChatGPT will ask you to prove the connection. Depending on the business, that can be a code to the business phone, a code to the business email, a DNS record on the website domain, or a connection to the Shopify store.

If OpenAI emailed you a claim link, open it while signed in as the person who received it. A forwarded link signed in under someone else's account is a common way this stalls.

A few listings are locked. If the screen says to contact support, that is the path. Do not keep retrying the same claim. Local shops and restaurants in the U.S. can currently claim a listing that already exists. Eligible online businesses may see a way to add one that ChatGPT does not have yet. Availability still depends on where the business is and what kind of business it is.

OpenAI reviews the claim and emails a decision, or a request for more information. You can cancel the request while it is in review.

If someone else has already claimed the business, open the profile, use the more-options menu, and dispute ownership. You will have to show, through Persona, that you are allowed to represent it.

## The claim is not the blue check

This is the step people skip, and then wonder why nothing publishes.

After the claim is approved, you still complete an identity check with Persona before you can publish changes, connect a product feed, see insights, or create a business agent. The blue check on the profile shows up after that Persona review is approved. Claiming the listing does not add the badge by itself.

Persona may ask for a government ID, business registration, and proof you can speak for the company. If you create a brand-new profile through Persona, approval can publish that profile immediately. There is no second "publish" button for the profile itself, so do not start that path until the name, address, and description are ready.

You can finish Persona later if you verified the claim another way. Open the Business tab, select the profile, and use the prompt to complete verification. Features that depend on Persona stay off until the approval email arrives. Sending the documents is not the same as being approved.

## Update the facts customers actually ask about

Once both reviews are approved, open the profile and correct the things a stranger would need: the website, a plain description, the category, the logo, and how delivery and returns work.

OpenAI reviews those edits. They can take a while to show up in a listing or in an answer, and ChatGPT can still draw on other sources. Saving a change does not mean every conversation updates that night.

If you sell products, connect a product feed so prices and availability stay current. Shopify can supply that feed. You can also upload a CSV that follows OpenAI's product feed specification. A stale price in the feed is worse than no feed, because ChatGPT will repeat it with confidence.

## Read the insights without fooling yourself

Insights cover the last 28 days, and only after Persona verification. Three numbers are worth knowing before you panic about a quiet dashboard.

For an online business, a mention counts when ChatGPT cites your website. Saying the brand name is not enough. For a local business, a mention counts when ChatGPT shows a result for that location. Website clicks are what they sound like. Discovery topics group the themes in answers that mentioned you. They are not the customer's prompts, and you never receive individual chats, including chats with your own agent.

A blank metric is not automatically zero. The activity may be under the reporting threshold, still collecting, or unavailable. If a number stays blank after you have been told reporting is on, that is a support question, not a verdict on the business.

## Publish the agent only after you try to break it

A business agent is optional, and it is not available to every listing you can claim. Eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the U.S. with a website can create one, if the industry also fits OpenAI's business agent policy. Some businesses will be able to manage a profile and still not be offered an agent.

If you connected Shopify, a draft agent may already be waiting. Open it. If not, create one from the Business tab after Persona is approved. You cannot create an agent while verification is pending or after it has failed.

Read what the agent will rely on: product details, the return policy, and any instructions you write for how it should answer. Then preview it with a question a real customer asks. "Can I return something I bought on sale?" is a better test than "Hello." If the answer is wrong, fix the source material and ask again.

Customers can talk to the agent only after you publish it. Until that button, it is a draft.

Be clear about the limit. The agent can explain products and policies and send people to your site. It cannot complete a purchase, take a booking, or look up a specific order. Those jobs still happen on your website.

## One brand at a time

Each brand is its own listing. Claiming the parent company does not claim the store down the street, and there is no single screen yet that runs every location together. If you operate more than one brand, plan on a separate claim for each.

Unpublished drafts can be deleted from the profile's overview menu. A published profile is a different matter. OpenAI Support will not unpublish or hide one as part of ordinary setup.

The click-path moves. When a button is not where you remember it, the current steps are in OpenAI's help article on [setting up your business on ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt). If you are stuck, contact OpenAI Support with the step you were on, the error text, and a screenshot that leaves out account numbers and codes.
